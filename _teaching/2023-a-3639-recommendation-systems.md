## Course description

A master’s-level course covering theoretical and practical approaches to personalised recommendation. The syllabus connects machine learning algorithms with evaluation methods, implementation tools, and research topics in recommender systems.

## Topics

- Collaborative filtering and evaluation of recommender systems.
- Matrix factorisation.
- Content-based recommendation and explainability.
- Hybrid recommendation approaches.
- Deep learning for recommendation systems.
