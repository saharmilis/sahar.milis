## Course description

A master’s-level introduction to natural language processing, combining linguistic, algorithmic, and statistical foundations with practical applications. The course covers classical language modelling and neural approaches to language understanding and generation.

## Topics

- Statistical language models, smoothing, evaluation, and part-of-speech tagging.
- Neural networks, word embeddings, and contextual representations.
- Recurrent networks, LSTMs, and GRUs.
- Attention mechanisms, transformers, BERT, GPT, and fine-tuning.
- Question answering, summarisation, and machine translation.
