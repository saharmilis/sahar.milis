## Course description

A practical introduction to data science for business, economics, and entrepreneurship. The course develops skills in Python-based data analysis and machine learning, with applications in predictive modelling, customer segmentation, and data-informed decision-making.

## Topics

- The data science lifecycle and its business applications.
- Data preparation, exploratory data analysis, and visualisation.
- Classification, regression, clustering, and predictive analytics.
- Recommendation systems and an introduction to deep learning.
- Text, image, time-series, and behavioural data.
