## Course description

An applied introduction to the data science workflow: framing a problem, acquiring and preparing data, building and evaluating models, and communicating findings. The course connects machine learning with business and entrepreneurship through practical AI applications and reproducible analysis.

## Topics

- Problem framing, the data science lifecycle, responsible AI, and reproducibility.
- Data cleaning, exploratory analysis, visualisation, and visual storytelling.
- Classification, regression, clustering, and model evaluation.
- Feature engineering, cross-validation, data leakage, and explainability.
- Recommendation systems, embeddings, and deep learning.
- Working with text, images, and multimodal data using pretrained models.
