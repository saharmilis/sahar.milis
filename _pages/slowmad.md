Places I've visited, worked, and travelled.

![World map showing places I've visited, worked, and travelled]({{ '/images/worldmap.png' | relative_url }})

Slomadism—or slow nomadism—is about building connections and getting to know a place beyond the surface. It lets me combine focused work with local life, trading the rush of traditional tourism for a slower, more deliberate rhythm. Staying in one location for months gives me time to settle into everyday routines—finding a favorite corner table at a café, discovering neighborhood markets, and balancing research with unhurried exploration.

This lifestyle keeps me grounded while expanding my worldview. Spending time in different cultures gives me opportunities to engage with local tech communities, meet people from different backgrounds, and see how other societies live and innovate. Moving intentionally gives me the mental space to write, code, and evolve, with fresh perspectives along the way. For me, slomadism is about making room for both meaningful work and the world outside my screen.
