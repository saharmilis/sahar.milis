My teaching at Reichman University focuses on building strong foundations in computer science and data science, with an emphasis on making complex theoretical concepts accessible through intuitive explanations, hands-on learning, and practical implementation.

Leading two courses:

  • Introduction to Data Science
  • Introduction to Computer Science

My teaching philosophy centers on bridging theory and practice, equipping students with the conceptual foundations, technical proficiency, and problem-solving skills to develop real-world applications, address complex computational challenges, and pursue independent academic research.

Course information, teaching materials, and syllabi are available below, organized by academic year and semester.

Guest Lectures & Industry Collaboration

An integral part of my courses is connecting students with industry experts across diverse technological domains, exposing them to real-world challenges, emerging innovations, and perspectives beyond the classroom.

Over the years, we have hosted guest speakers from leading technology companies and startups, including:

NVIDIA · Meta · Microsoft · Palo Alto Networks · VIZ · Dream · Darrow · BioCatch · Aidoc · Bright Data · Lemonade, and others.

We welcome industry professionals, researchers, and technology leaders interested in sharing their expertise through guest lectures (±30 minutes, English/Hebrew). Topics may include company and product overviews, technical innovations, ongoing research, industry challenges, and career insights.

Interested in delivering a guest lecture or exploring academic–industry collaboration? Please reach out to discuss potential topics and arrangements.

Visiting Students & Course Auditing

We welcome visiting students, guest attendees, prospective auditors, non-degree students, and lifelong learners interested in attending individual lectures or auditing a course. Those interested in sitting in on a lecture, continuing education, informal attendance, academic tourism, sit-ins, or class crashing - are welcome.

Please contact the teaching team or the university in advance to discuss availability and arrangements. All participation is subject to university policies and approval.
